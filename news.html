World Cup Outsider Watch

Ecuador: the 100/1 outsiders nobody will want to face

If you want a team that is fundamentally built to grind through a knockout tournament, look at Ecuador. They are priced as massive outsiders, but their qualification campaign tells a completely different story.

The Form

Ecuador quietly finished 2nd in the brutal CONMEBOL qualifying table, finishing ahead of both Brazil and Uruguay.

The Defensive Rock

They have arguably one of the most disciplined, physically imposing defensive structures in international football right now. Across 18 gruelling qualifying matches in South America, they conceded only 5 goals.

Why They Are Dangerous

Ecuador are an absolute nightmare to play against. They boast a world-class spine, with Moisés Caicedo anchoring the midfield, shielded by Paris Saint-Germain’s Willian Pacho and Arsenal’s Piero Hincapié.

Add in generational teenage talents like Kendry Páez, and they have the creative spark to punish teams once they have frustrated opponents into making mistakes.

ScoreCast24 verdict: Ecuador may not win the World Cup, but they are exactly the kind of side that could ruin a favourite’s tournament.

Honourable Mention: Turkey (Odds: ~100/1)

If you prefer an unpredictable, high-ceiling maverick team, Vincenzo Montella's Turkey are worth watching.

They are incredibly streaky, but they possess two of the most exciting young attacking players in Europe: Arda Güler of Real Madrid and Kenan Yıldız of Juventus.

Under Hakan Çalhanoğlu's leadership, if those young stars click, Turkey can outscore almost anyone on their day.

ScoreCast24 verdict: Turkey are the ultimate wildcard. Their inconsistency makes them difficult to trust, but their attacking talent means they could be one of the tournament's most entertaining teams.
