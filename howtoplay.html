ScoreCast24 Rules

ScoreCast24 is a football score prediction game where players predict the final scorelines of selected fixtures.

How to Play

Each round will contain a set of fixtures, usually including Premier League and Championship games.

Key matches from other competitions or European leagues may also be added, such as El Clásico, the Old Firm Derby or other major fixtures.

Predict the scoreline for each fixture before the prediction deadline closes.

Once predictions have been submitted, they cannot be changed.

You should only submit one set of predictions per round. Duplicate or suspicious entries may be removed.

Rounds

One round of games will generally run from Friday to Monday, or from Tuesday to Thursday when there are midweek fixtures.

Final results for a round will be confirmed once the last game in that round has been played.

Games that are postponed will score no points.

Scoring System

Correct exact scoreline: 5 points

Correctly predicted draw: 3 points

Correctly predicted away win: 2 points

Correctly predicted home win: 1 point

Incorrect result: 0 points

Example

If you predict 2-3 and the actual score is 1-2, you score 2 points because you correctly predicted an away win.

If the actual score is exactly 2-3, you score 5 points.

Prediction Deadline

Predictions must be submitted before the deadline shown on the homepage. Once the deadline has passed, entries will be locked.

Late entries will not be accepted.

Tiebreak Rules

If two or more players finish on the same number of points, the winner will be decided in this order:

1. The player with the most exact scoreline predictions.

2. The player with the most correct results overall.

3. The player who submitted their predictions earliest.

Results and Leaderboard

The leaderboard will update after match results have been added.

Final round standings will be confirmed once every fixture in that round has been completed.

Fair Play

ScoreCast24 is intended to be a fun prediction game. Any attempt to manipulate entries, submit multiple usernames, or exploit bugs may result in removal from the leaderboard.

Development

ScoreCast24 is still in development. Bugs, display issues, scoring errors or rule changes may occur while the game is being improved.

Feedback is welcome and will help improve future versions.
