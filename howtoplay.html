This is currently a game in progress. We are testing out the game using the World Cup, and any bugs or feedback can be identified in time for the English 2026/27 football season.

How to Play

Simply predict the outcome of each round of football results.

You will score 5 points for a correct scoreline prediction.

If you correctly predict a draw you will score 3 points.

If you correctly predict an away win you will score 2 points.

If you correctly predict a home win you will score 1 point.

For example, if you predict 2-3 and the score is 1-2, you will score 2 points because you correctly predicted an away win.

If the score is exactly 2-3, you will score 5 points.

For the purpose of the World Cup test phase, the team listed second in the official fixtures will be classified as the away team.
