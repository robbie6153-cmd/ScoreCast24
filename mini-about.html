About Dream Team Mini Leagues

Mini Leagues let you compete against friends, colleagues and other football supporters throughout the season.

What are Mini Leagues?

Mini Leagues give you the opportunity to compete against friends, colleagues or like-minded football supporters.

For a £1 creation fee you can create your own Mini League with space for up to 20 players.

As your league grows, additional places can be added so your competition can continue to expand.

🌍 Public Mini Leagues

Public Mini Leagues are open for any ScoreCast24 user to join.

We recommend choosing a clear league name so players know who the league is intended for.

Examples:

  • Liverpool FC Supporters
  • Manchester United Fans
  • NHS Employees
  • Teachers League
  • Five-a-Side Friends

Each new league begins with space for 20 players.

When your league becomes full, any member can purchase an additional 10 spaces for £1. There is no limit to how many times this can be done.

🔒 Private Mini Leagues

Private Mini Leagues operate in exactly the same way but can only be joined by invitation from an existing member.

The league creator can initially invite up to 20 players.

Once someone has joined the league, they can also invite other people to join the league.

If the league becomes full, any member can purchase an additional 10 spaces for £1. There is no limit to how many times this can be done.

Private leagues are ideal for:

  • Friends
  • Families
  • Work colleagues
  • Sports teams
  • Supporters' groups

League Rules

  • Every player uses the normal ScoreCast24 Dream Team scoring system.
  • League tables update automatically as Dream Team scores are calculated.
  • Mini League results are based solely on Dream Team points.
  • The standard ScoreCast24 Terms and Rules apply to all Mini Leagues.
  • ScoreCast24 reserves the right to remove offensive or inappropriate league names.
