Create a private Dream Team Mini League for friends, family or work colleagues.

How Private Leagues Work

Private Mini Leagues are hidden from the public and can only be joined after receiving an invitation from an existing member.

Creating a private league costs £1 and includes space for up to 20 players.

Members can search for another ScoreCast24 username and send them an invitation to join the league.

The invited player will see the invitation when they next log in and can choose to accept or decline it.

Once a player has accepted and joined the league, they can also invite other ScoreCast24 users.

When the league reaches capacity, any existing member can purchase an additional 10 places for £1.

League Rules

  • Players can only join after accepting an invitation from an existing league member.
  • League members can search for ScoreCast24 usernames and send invitations.
  • An invitation does not use a league space until it has been accepted.
  • A user cannot join the same league more than once.
  • Invitations may be accepted or declined by the invited user.
  • All Dream Team scoring uses the standard ScoreCast24 scoring system.
  • League tables update automatically after weekly scores are calculated.
  • ScoreCast24 may remove offensive or inappropriate league names.
