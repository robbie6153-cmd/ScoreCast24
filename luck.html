The Greatest Football Nations Denied World Cup Glory

From Dutch heartbreak to Zambia’s tragic near-miss, some footballing nations have left a huge mark on the game without ever lifting — or even reaching — the World Cup.

The Netherlands: the greatest never to win it

The greatest footballing nation never to win the World Cup is, without doubt, the Netherlands. Twice finalists in the 1970s, they dazzled the world with their unique brand of total football, led by world-class talents such as Johan Cruyff and Johan Neeskens.

Another golden generation emerged with the 1988 European champions, featuring legendary names such as Marco van Basten, Ruud Gullit and Frank Rijkaard.

And if you think England have been unlucky with penalty shootouts, the Netherlands have suffered plenty of World Cup heartbreak of their own. They went out on penalties against Brazil in the 1998 semi-final, Argentina in the 2014 semi-final, and Argentina again in the 2022 quarter-final.

A star-studded Dutch side featuring Jaap Stam, Dennis Bergkamp, Frank de Boer, Jimmy Floyd Hasselbaink and Patrick Kluivert reached the semi-final in 1998, but then failed to qualify for the 2002 tournament.

They finally reached another final in 2010, with Robin van Persie and Arjen Robben leading the line, only to lose 1–0 to Spain after extra time in a famously bad-tempered match. They reached the quarter-finals again in 2022, but shockingly failed to qualify altogether in 2018.

Zambia: the best never to play at a World Cup?

When it comes to qualifying for a World Cup, perhaps the best country never to appear on the tournament stage is Zambia.

In terms of footballing pedigree, historical success and elite player production, Zambia is widely regarded as one of the strongest football nations never to make it to a World Cup.

Zambia won the Africa Cup of Nations in 2012 and finished as runners-up in 1974 and 1994. They have consistently been a major force in African football, producing legendary talent such as Kalusha Bwalya, the 1988 African Footballer of the Year, and modern stars such as Patson Daka.

The tragedy of 1993

Zambia’s greatest team was tragically lost in the 1993 plane crash that killed 18 players and members of the coaching staff.

Despite having to rebuild a completely new squad from scratch in a matter of months, Zambia came agonisingly close to qualifying for the 1994 World Cup, falling just one point short.

Honourable mention: Finland

Finland, known as the Eagle-Owls, have also produced genuine world-class icons of the game. Jari Litmanen won the Champions League with Ajax and later played for Barcelona and Liverpool, while Sami Hyypiä became a legendary Liverpool captain.

Finland finally broke their major tournament duck by qualifying for Euro 2020, but the World Cup remains elusive.
