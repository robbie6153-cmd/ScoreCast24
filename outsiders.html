World Cup Outsider Watch

Teams that could spring a surprise at the 2026 World Cup.

Ecuador: The 100/1 Outsiders Nobody Will Want To Face

If you want a team that is fundamentally built to grind through a knockout tournament, look at Ecuador. They are priced as massive outsiders, but their qualification campaign tells a completely different story.

The Form

Ecuador quietly finished second in the brutal CONMEBOL qualifying table, ahead of both Brazil and Uruguay.

The Defensive Rock

They possess one of the most disciplined and physically imposing defensive structures in international football. Across 18 gruelling South American qualifiers they conceded only five goals.

Why They Are Dangerous

Ecuador are an absolute nightmare to play against. Moisés Caicedo anchors the midfield, while Paris Saint-Germain's Willian Pacho and Arsenal's Piero Hincapié provide quality at the back.

Add teenage star Kendry Páez and they have enough creativity to punish opponents once frustration starts to set in.

ScoreCast24 Verdict: Ecuador may not win the World Cup, but they are exactly the kind of side capable of ending a favourite's tournament.

Honourable Mention: Türkiye (100/1)

If you prefer an unpredictable, high-ceiling maverick team, Vincenzo Montella's Türkiye are worth watching.

They possess two of the most exciting young attacking talents in Europe: Arda Güler of Real Madrid and Kenan Yıldız of Juventus.

Under Hakan Çalhanoğlu's leadership, if those young stars click, Türkiye can outscore almost anybody.

ScoreCast24 Verdict: Türkiye are the ultimate wildcard. Their inconsistency makes them difficult to trust, but their attacking talent could make them one of the tournament's most entertaining sides.

Norway: The Ultimate Dark Horse?

After 28 years of waiting, Norway is finally back on football's biggest stage for the 2026 World Cup. Backed by a Golden Generation and immense momentum, they are widely viewed as one of the tournament's most dangerous dark horses.

A Perfect Qualifying Campaign

Norway didn't just qualify for the World Cup; they completely battered the door down. Ståle Solbakken's side achieved a 100% win record in UEFA Group I, winning all eight of their qualifying matches.

They scored an astonishing 37 goals and conceded just five. Their perfect campaign included statement victories over Italy, beating the four-time world champions 3-0 in Oslo and 4-1 at the San Siro.

Erling Haaland: The Goal Machine

At just 25 years old, Erling Haaland arrives at his first major international tournament as Norway's all-time leading goalscorer. He scored an incredible 16 goals during qualifying.

Haaland matched Robert Lewandowski's UEFA qualifying record and arrives in North America as one of the favourites for the Golden Boot.

Martin Ødegaard Pulling The Strings

If Haaland is the sword, Martin Ødegaard is the architect. The Norwegian captain provides the vision, creativity and composure that makes Norway such a dangerous attacking force.

Alongside Alexander Sørloth, Ødegaard helps form one of the most exciting forward units outside the traditional favourites.

ScoreCast24 Verdict: Norway are no longer just a team with one superstar. They possess quality throughout the squad and arrive with genuine belief. A quarter-final run or better would not be a shock.
