About ScoreCast24

ScoreCast24 is a football prediction platform inspired by a simple score prediction game that has been played in British prisons, workplaces, pubs, social clubs and groups of friends for many years.

The traditional game is straightforward. Players predict the scores from a round of football fixtures. Points are awarded depending on the accuracy of those predictions, and once every match has finished, the player with the highest score wins the round.

Despite its simplicity, the game creates genuine excitement. A weekend of football can come down to one final kick-off, one late goal or one unexpected result. Every fixture matters, and every goal can change the leaderboard.

ScoreCast24 takes that classic paper-based prediction game and brings it online. Players can submit predictions, follow results, compare scores and compete on leaderboards throughout the football season.

The platform currently focuses on weekly football score predictions, including Premier League and Championship fixtures. Featured matches from domestic cups, European competitions, international football and major rivalries may also be added from time to time.

ScoreCast24 is developed by RobTechUK as an independent football prediction platform. The aim is to keep the game simple, competitive and easy to understand, while gradually adding new features for football fans.

We have also developed a weekly Dream Team game, where you can select a weekly team draft every week, keep your existing squad or change players every week. This adds a unique twist to the traditional Dream Team games.

Dream Team and ScoreCast24 are constantly being reviewed and improved upon. As a new game, things may not always run smoothly and some results may be late, but we are constantly working on this and are also open to suggestions on ways to improve.

Thank you for your patience. If you have any suggestions, don't hesitate to contact us at RobTechUK@hotmail.com.

Whether you study every fixture or simply enjoy guessing the scores, ScoreCast24 gives you the chance to test your football knowledge against other players.

Thank you for visiting ScoreCast24, and good luck with your predictions.
