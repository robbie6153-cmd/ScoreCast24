ScoreCast24

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScoreCast24 collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you use the ScoreCast24 website, create an account, enter games or competitions, make payments, or otherwise interact with the service.

ScoreCast24 is operated by RobTechUK.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ScoreCast24, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address used for account registration, verification, login and account recovery
  • Username used on leaderboards and within ScoreCast24 games
  • Football predictions and competition entries
  • Dream Team selections and related game data
  • Submission dates and timestamps
  • Leaderboard scores, rankings and results
  • Payment status, payment reference information and transaction information relating to paid competitions or features
  • Information generated when you interact with the website, such as browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited and usage data
  • Technical information that may be necessary for security, fraud prevention and website operation

Please do not use personal information such as your full name, phone number, email address or home address as your public username.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create, verify and manage user accounts
  • Allow users to log in securely
  • Store football predictions and Dream Team entries
  • Calculate scores and generate leaderboards
  • Operate paid competitions and other paid features
  • Confirm whether payments have been successfully completed
  • Prevent duplicate, fraudulent or suspicious submissions
  • Protect the security and integrity of the website
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Analyse website usage and improve ScoreCast24
  • Display and measure advertising where applicable
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, accounting or financial obligations

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful reason for processing personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, ScoreCast24 may rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Contract – where processing is necessary to provide the ScoreCast24 service you have requested, including managing your account, accepting entries and processing paid features.
  • Legitimate interests – where necessary to operate, secure, improve and protect ScoreCast24, prevent fraud and understand how the service is being used.
  • Consent – where you have given permission for certain optional technologies, including where consent is required for cookies, analytics or advertising.
  • Legal obligation – where we need to process or retain information in order to comply with applicable laws or regulatory requirements.

4. Public Information

ScoreCast24 is a competitive football prediction service. Certain information may therefore be visible to other users.

This may include:

  • Your username
  • Your scores
  • Your leaderboard position
  • Your football predictions
  • Your Dream Team selections

Your account email address is not displayed publicly on ScoreCast24.

5. Payments and Stripe

ScoreCast24 uses Stripe to process payments for paid competitions and other paid features.

When you make a payment, payment information is submitted securely to Stripe. ScoreCast24 does not receive or store your full debit or credit card number.

Stripe may process information such as:

  • Payment card information
  • Transaction amount
  • Payment status
  • Billing or contact information where applicable
  • Device, fraud prevention and security information

ScoreCast24 may receive limited information from Stripe confirming details such as whether a payment succeeded, the amount paid and a Stripe transaction or payment reference.

Stripe processes personal information in accordance with its own privacy policies and legal obligations.

You can read Stripe's privacy policy at:

Stripe Privacy Policy

6. Account and Database Services

ScoreCast24 uses Google Firebase services to provide account authentication, database storage and other website functionality.

Information stored using Firebase may include:

  • User account identifiers
  • Email addresses
  • Usernames
  • Prediction entries
  • Dream Team entries
  • Scores and leaderboard information
  • Submission timestamps

Password authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. ScoreCast24 does not store users' passwords in its own website database in readable form.

7. Football Data Services

ScoreCast24 may use third-party football data services and APIs to obtain information such as fixtures, results, player statistics, player ratings and match information.

This information is primarily sporting data rather than personal information about ScoreCast24 users. ScoreCast24 does not intentionally provide user account information to football data providers unless this is necessary for the operation of a particular service.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ScoreCast24 may use cookies, browser storage and similar technologies to operate the website, remember settings, provide security, understand website usage and support advertising.

Some technologies are necessary for the operation of the website. Other technologies, such as certain analytics or advertising technologies, may require your consent.

Where required, non-essential cookies or similar technologies should only be used after you have been given an appropriate choice.

9. Google Analytics and Advertising

ScoreCast24 may use Google services including Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

These services may process information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device and browser information
  • Interaction with the website
  • Advertising identifiers or cookie information where permitted

This information may be used to measure website traffic, understand how visitors use ScoreCast24, measure advertising performance and, where permitted, display relevant advertising.

Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies.

10. How We Share Information

ScoreCast24 does not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be shared with third parties where reasonably necessary to operate the service.

These may include:

  • Google Firebase for authentication and database services
  • Stripe for payment processing
  • Google Analytics for website analytics
  • Google AdSense and related advertising services
  • Hosting, infrastructure and security providers
  • Football data providers where necessary to operate football-related features
  • Professional advisers, regulators, courts or law enforcement where disclosure is legally required

11. International Data Transfers

Some of the third-party services used by ScoreCast24 operate internationally.

As a result, personal information may sometimes be processed or stored outside the United Kingdom.

Where this occurs, the relevant provider is responsible for using appropriate safeguards where required by applicable data protection law.

12. Data Retention

ScoreCast24 retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

Account information, predictions, Dream Team entries and scores may be retained while an account remains active and afterwards where necessary to:

  • Maintain historical competition results
  • Maintain leaderboard records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Meet accounting or legal obligations
  • Protect the legitimate interests of ScoreCast24

Payment and transaction information may be retained for longer periods where this is required for financial, tax, accounting or legal purposes.

13. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

These measures include the use of established third-party authentication, database and payment providers.

However, no website, internet transmission or online storage system can guarantee absolute security.

14. Your Data Protection Rights

If UK data protection law applies to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, these may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Request transfer of certain information where the right to data portability applies
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to exemptions or legal requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details at the bottom of this policy.

15. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your ScoreCast24 account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Certain information may need to be retained after account deletion where necessary for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Public competition records may also need to be retained in an anonymised or limited form in order to preserve the integrity of historical leaderboards and competition results.

16. Children

ScoreCast24 is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

Certain competitions or paid features may have additional age restrictions which will be stated in the applicable competition rules or terms.

17. Third-Party Websites

ScoreCast24 may contain links to third-party websites or services.

ScoreCast24 is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites operated by other organisations. You should review their privacy policies before providing personal information.
