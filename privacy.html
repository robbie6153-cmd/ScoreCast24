Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ScoreCast24 collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

ScoreCast24 currently collects the information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Usernames
  • Prediction entries
  • Submission timestamps

We do not currently collect payment information.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Store prediction entries
  • Generate leaderboards
  • Prevent duplicate submissions
  • Improve the website and user experience

Cookies and Analytics

ScoreCast24 may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the website and to improve performance.

If analytics services are added in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Storage

Prediction data is stored using secure third-party services, including Firebase by Google.

While reasonable steps are taken to protect data, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing

ScoreCast24 does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Information may only be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the security and integrity of the website.

Public Information

Usernames and leaderboard positions are publicly visible to other users.

Please do not use personal information such as your full name, email address, phone number, or home address as your username.

Your Rights

You may contact us to request information about the data associated with your username or to request its removal where practical.

Children

ScoreCast24 is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected on this page.
