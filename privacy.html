Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ScoreCast24 collects, how it is used, how it is stored, and the choices you have regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

ScoreCast24 may collect the following information:

  • Email address, used for account registration and login
  • Username, used on leaderboards and across ScoreCast24 games
  • Prediction entries
  • Submission timestamps
  • Leaderboard scores and rankings
  • Basic technical information such as device, browser and usage data

Please do not use personal information such as your full name, phone number, email address or home address as your public username.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Store prediction entries
  • Generate leaderboards
  • Prevent duplicate or suspicious submissions
  • Improve the website and user experience
  • Help protect the security and integrity of the website

3. Public Information

Usernames, scores, rankings and prediction entries may be visible to other users of ScoreCast24.

Your email address is not displayed publicly on the website.

4. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

ScoreCast24 may use cookies, analytics tools and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the website, improve performance and support advertising.

ScoreCast24 uses Google services, including Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services may collect usage data and may use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic, improve services and display relevant advertisements.

5. Data Storage

User account information, usernames and prediction data are stored using secure third-party cloud services, including Google Firebase Authentication and Google Firestore.

While reasonable steps are taken to protect your data, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Payments

ScoreCast24 does not currently store full payment card details.

If paid competitions, credits or other paid features are introduced, payments will be processed securely by a third-party payment provider. Payment card details will be handled by the payment provider and not stored directly by ScoreCast24.

7. Data Sharing

ScoreCast24 does not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared only where necessary to operate the website, process payments, provide analytics or advertising services, comply with the law, or protect the security and integrity of ScoreCast24.

8. Data Retention

We retain account information and prediction data for as long as needed to operate ScoreCast24, maintain leaderboards, resolve disputes, improve the service, or meet legal requirements.

You may request deletion of your account or personal information by contacting us.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK data protection law, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask for corrections, request deletion, or object to certain uses of your data.

To make a request, please contact us using the details below.

10. Children

ScoreCast24 is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected on this page.
